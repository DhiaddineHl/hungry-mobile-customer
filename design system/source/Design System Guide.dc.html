06

States.

Every screen implements loading, error, empty and success with these patterns. Examples on the board, section 4a.

LoadingSkeleton in the page’s own layout; spinner inside the button for actions. Chrome stays usable.
ErrorRed field + message, or a red banner above what failed. Always say what to do next; keep the user’s input.
Empty72 pt icon well, one line of why, at most one action.
SuccessNavy toast (Undo when reversible), or a success screen at the end of a flow.
