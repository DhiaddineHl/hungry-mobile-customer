When things load, fail or finish.

One set of patterns for every page. Content loads as skeletons in the page’s own layout; actions load inside their button. Errors sit where they happen: a red field with a message, or a red banner above the affected content. Success is a short navy toast, or a full screen when the step ends a flow.

1aHungry · Home & search · 390×844 · follows auth system

Less shelf, more appetite.

The current home screen stacks six sections with competing headings, icon rows and three different card styles. This version has a clear order: where the food is going, a greeting in the brand voice, search, then browsing. Every restaurant uses one of two components: a large card for browsing, a compact row for results. All images, category illustrations and tab icons come from the Figma file.

H1–H3

Home.

Order. Address first, because it decides what is available. Then "Hungry, Ahmad?", then search and browsing. The "?" echoes the logo.

Less noise. Each restaurant's rating, time and fee sit on one text line instead of three coloured icons. A promotion appears as a single navy tag, and the delivery fee is only highlighted when it's free.

Scroll. The address and search stay pinned at the top. Categories and filters combine, and active filters show a × so they are easy to clear.
